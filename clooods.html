Assemble and fasten

The elements of the Clooods modular sofa are connected with velcro fastenings placed between the frame and the seat cushions. The double velcro fastenings ensure the sofa won't come apart even when used as a bed, and they're incredibly easy to use. In just a few moments, your sofa transforms into a corner sofa, a two-seater, multiple armchairs, a bed, and more! Clooods is seating tailored to your needs – in every situation!

— Still unsure?

Questions everyone asks before buying.

How do I know the sofa will fit my room?▾

You don't have to guess. You'll find the exact dimensions further up this page. Order free fabric samples, plan your sofa module by module in the configurator, or experience it live in one of our showrooms – so you decide with confidence, not hope.

Can I add or rearrange modules later?▾

Yes. Thanks to the special connection system, corners, armchairs and ottomans can be added or rearranged at any time – your Clooods grows along with your home.

Can the sofa withstand kids and pets?▾

Yes. The covers are removable and washable, and the sturdy beechwood frame is built for everyday life – including kids, pets, and guests.

Is it worth the investment?▾

Clooods is built modularly: instead of replacing the whole sofa, you only renew individual parts or covers – making it more cost-effective in the long run than a classic sofa.

What if I'm not sure?▾

Order free fabric samples, book a free consultation, or visit one of our showrooms – we'll help you make the right decision.
